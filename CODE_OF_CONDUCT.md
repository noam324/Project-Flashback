\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Standard



Project Flashback is intended to be a constructive and respectful open-source project.



Participants are expected to:



\* Be respectful and professional.

\* Focus on technical issues and constructive discussion.

\* Accept reasonable differences of opinion.

\* Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior.

\* Keep discussions relevant to the project.



\## Unacceptable Behavior



Unacceptable behavior includes:



\* Harassment

\* Threats

\* Personal attacks

\* Hate speech

\* Deliberate disruption

\* Publishing private information

\* Malicious behavior toward contributors or users



Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a safe and constructive project environment.
